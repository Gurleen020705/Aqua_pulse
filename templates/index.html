HOW TO READ A CHECK

A clear result, and what to do next

The model does not look at one number alone. It compares oxygen, temperature, pH, nitrate and phosphorus together, then returns a health signal with confidence.

Healthy

The five measurements together look like waters that supported life in the GEMStat record. Keep routine monitoring and share the observation with your group.

At-Risk

The mix of values is closer to stressed sites. Re-sample within 24 hours, look for nearby runoff, and tell local water managers if it persists.

Use it as an early warning

This is a field screen, not a certified lab result. Unusual weather, a dirty probe, or values far from the training range can all change the picture.
